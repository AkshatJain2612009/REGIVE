Turn your surplus into someone's resource.

ReGive connects individuals, restaurants, and businesses with NGOs to ensure usable items and surplus food reach those who need them most.

How ReGive Works

1. Pin & List

Users pin their location and list items like clothes, books, or surplus food.

2. NGO Match

Verified NGOs see the listings in real-time and claim the items.

3. Collect & Distribute

Resources are collected and delivered to families in need, reducing waste.
